The short version

Bird Hop Deluxe is free. It has no ads, no accounts and no in-app purchases. It does not collect personal data, and your progress stays on your device.

What the game saves on your device

To remember your progress and settings, the game saves your best score, coins, lifetime stats, badges, missions, challenges, daily streak, unlocked worlds and items, and your settings (sound, haptics, game mode and graphics quality). It also keeps a few other progress flags, such as whether you have seen the ending.

This data stays in the app's private storage on your device. It is excluded from Android backups, and it is never sent to us or to anyone else.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone input, advertising ID or usage analytics. The app contains no analytics or crash reporting code.

Internet access

The app does not request the internet permission, so it cannot send or receive data over the internet. Everything it needs is built into the app, and it works fully offline.

Permissions

  • Vibrate is used for haptic feedback. You can turn it off with the Haptics button in the menu.
  • Prevent device from sleeping keeps the screen on during play.

The app requests no other permissions.

Ads and third parties

The game has no ads. It uses no ad networks, analytics tools, social logins or third-party SDKs. We do not sell or share data, because the app does not collect any.

Google Play and Android

The app is downloaded through Google Play, which has its own privacy policy. If you have chosen to share usage and diagnostics data with app developers in your Android settings, Google may send us anonymous crash and performance reports for this app.

Android can back up device data to your own Google account, depending on your settings. This app's game data is excluded from those backups.

Children

Bird Hop Deluxe is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Your game data lives only on your device, so you control it. To delete it, uninstall the app, or open Android Settings, then Apps, then Bird Hop Deluxe, then Storage, and tap Clear storage.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of the page. The version shown on this page is the one that applies.

Contact

For questions about this policy, use the developer email address shown on the Google Play listing for Bird Hop Deluxe.
